abstract: 'T-CLIP adapts CLIP to thermal imagery, where visible-spectrum representations struggle to connect images with text. The work introduces IR-Cap, a physics-aware captioning pipeline and dataset with both scene-level and object-level descriptions. A decoupled dual-LoRA framework learns these complementary representations separately, addressing conflicts between overall scene context and local heat signatures. Experiments across three thermal benchmarks show improved cross-modal retrieval, alongside an exploratory application to text-conditioned thermal image generation.'
